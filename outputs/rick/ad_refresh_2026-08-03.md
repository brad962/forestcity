## Context

Day 69 since launch. August 3 — one week into August. Last week's refresh retired all July language and pivoted to "August is here." This week the pivot is complete: we're not announcing August anymore, we're living in it.

Five active triggers for the week of August 3:

1. **School starts in 15 days** — NE Ohio districts start August 18–25. The generic "before school starts" hook is now a specific countdown. 4. **Realtor August listing crunch — code red** — August 1 was the soft deadline for homes going on market in August. Any realtor-referred lead who hasn't booked is at maximum urgency. These respond to specific slots, not general messaging.
5. **Competitor re-growth concern spreading** — Marcus's July 27 VOC: "My neighbor's re-grew within a month — that's why I wanted to go with someone who uses the right stuff." Word-of-mouth soft-wash education is actively spreading through NE Ohio neighborhoods. This objection is a warm lead in disguise — they already believe the wrong company will fail them.
