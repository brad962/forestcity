## The Three Opens — August 10

### Open #1 — Brothers Overflow Capture (PEAK — Mon–Wed Only)

This is the single highest-leverage conversion opportunity of the entire summer. The Brothers-overflow customer exists in volume right now, they're actively searching, and the conversion path is short: GBP → reviews → call → slot. The only thing that kills this conversion is a slow response or not having availability to offer.

**Tommy protocol for this customer (Mon–Wed):**
- Inbound calls: slot offer in sentence two. Don't wait for them to ask.
- If full before Aug 18: immediately offer September. "We're tight before school but I can put you in the first week of September." Don't lose the customer to the competition just because the school deadline has passed.
- Review: is Forest City's Google Business Profile completely up to date? Correct service areas listed? Photo count? Any recent negative reviews without owner replies? This is the conversion page for Brothers-overflow customers and it needs to be spotless.

### Open #2 — Fall-Prep First Mover (Opens Now, Peaks September)

No competitor has been observed launching fall-prep content. The window to own "before it gets cold" in NE Ohio is this week. First mover advantage is real in a market where most competitors are passive — the frame the first company establishes tends to persist because passive competitors never counter it.

**The fall-prep frame for Forest City:**
- "September is the other great window."
- "Before the leaves, before the cold, before the calendar gets full again."
- "NE Ohio fall weather can turn fast — August is the safest window. September if you have to."

This doesn't require complex creative. A simple before/after with "Before it gets cold — September slots opening" runs well as a Facebook post and a Tommy close. The key is being the first Cleveland power washing company to say it out loud, in public, in this week.

### Open #3 — September Listing Capture (Intensifying Now)

September listings are going active. Realtor-driven pre-sale customers who need exterior photos in the first week of September are booking this week. This is a high-LTV customer: they're buying before a transaction, so quality and timing matter more than price; they're often willing to do the full exterior package; and they can generate a realtor referral relationship.

**Danny's realtor outreach priority:**
