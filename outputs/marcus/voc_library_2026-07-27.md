## The "Summer's Almost Gone" Emotional Frame — Late July Specific

The dominant emotional shift from July 27 onward is **summer regret**, not summer urgency. Customers aren't motivating toward something — they're moving away from the feeling that they let the whole summer slip.

**Language that converts this customer:**

- "July went fast. We know. We still have August — and it looks just as good after a clean as it did in May. Book now while we can still get you in."
- "You've been meaning to do it all summer. August starts Thursday. We have Thursday afternoon and Friday open — want one of those?"
- "Three weeks until school. Most of our August customers say the same thing: 'I wish I'd done this in June.' You're not in June anymore, but you're not in September yet. This week still counts."

Tommy: this customer responds to **empathy before the close**, not just the date. Acknowledge the summer went fast, then give them the specific slot. The two-step close outperforms the straight pitch with this emotional profile.
