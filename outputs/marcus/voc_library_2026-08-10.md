## What's New This Update

August 10. School starts in 8 days for most NE Ohio districts. This is the last week.

**The August 10 mindset shift:**

The emotional landscape this week is sharper and more divided than any prior week this summer. Two distinct customer personalities have separated out completely:

- **The final-week sprinter.** They've been watching the school-start deadline get closer for two weeks. They are ready to act *right now* if you make it dead simple. "School starts August 18 — can we fit them in before then?" has a yes/no answer and Tommy should be leading every text with a slot offer, not a question.
- **The "I missed it" customer.** They wanted it done before school but didn't move fast enough, or heard "we're full" somewhere, or just accepted they're not going to make it. They haven't stopped wanting the work done — they've just mentally moved the deadline to "before the weather turns." This is Forest City's pivot customer for the second half of August. The right message is not urgency; it's transition: "School's almost here. Fall is the other great window. Here's when we can do it."

Key dynamics active this week:

- **The school-start window is closing — but it hasn't closed.** August 10–14 is the final sprint. Any customer who books this week and gets in before August 18 is a win. Tommy should be working the warm list hard Mon–Wed. The window doesn't formally close until next Monday, but realistically customers who book Thursday–Friday are squeezed.
- **"I wanted it done before school but you're probably too busy"** — this is the incoming message from procrastinator-acknowledged customers. They assume you're booked. The correct response dismantles the assumption before anything else: give the customer a specific open slot immediately. "We actually have Thursday the 13th. That still works before school starts." The assumption of your unavailability is costing you easy conversions.
- **Fall-prep is now a legitimate booking frame, not just a hint.** The phrase "before it gets cold" now lands differently than it did on August 3. In early August, it felt distant. On August 10 — a week from back-to-school, seven weeks from peak fall color — it's real. This is the week that frame crosses from emerging to active. Plant it in every close.
- **September listing season is officially open.** September listings are going active now. Homes going on the market the first week of September need exterior photos within two weeks. The pre-sale customer is either booking this week for a before-August-18 job or booking this week for a first-week-of-September job. Either way, they're booking this week.
- **"Last summer job"** — a small but distinct customer segment emerges in the second week of August who frames this as their end-of-summer home project. They're in a seasonal completion mindset: finishing up summer to-dos before fall routines restart. The emotional note is satisfaction and closure, not urgency. "I want to close out summer with a clean house" is a real booking motivator.
- **Extreme heat close.** Northeast Ohio has been running high humidity and heat through August. August 10 algae growth on north-facing siding and shaded driveways is at absolute peak for the year. Any homeowner who looks at the north side of their house this week will see maximum contrast between clean and dirty.

## The "I Assumed You Were Booked" Customer

This is the highest-leverage conversion this week. The customer who almost didn't call because they assumed availability was gone — and then did call, got a slot, and booked — is a close that happens because Tommy *leads with the slot*, not the pitch.

**The wrong script:**
"Thanks for calling Forest City Power Washing. How can I help you today?"
[Customer explains they want it done before school]
"Okay great — let me check our calendar. Hm, we're getting pretty tight..."

**The right script:**
"Thanks for calling. You're lucky you caught us — we have Thursday the 13th still open before school starts. Is that going to work for you?"

Tommy should lead every inbound call this week with a slot offer. Not a question. Not an availability check. A specific date, offered immediately. The psychological dynamic: the customer's assumption of your unavailability makes finding availability feel like a win. Give them the win in sentence two.

The same logic applies to texts: "Hey, saw your message. We have Thursday the 13th before school — want it?" beats "What day were you thinking?" by a significant conversion margin on August 10.
