**Post-window debrief:**
The Clean Pro damage window is closed. The question now is: did they respond? Competitors who take review damage seriously launch one of three counterattacks within 7–14 days: (1) owner replies on GBP, often formulaic but sometimes revealing; (2) satisfaction guarantee or pricing transparency language added to the website; (3) reputation management service generating fresh 5-star reviews.

**When Bradley runs Marcus locally — Clean Pro August audit:**
1. Check GBP for owner replies on any 1–2 star reviews from the last 30 days. Tone and content reveal their defensive posture.
2. Check their website for any new "pricing guarantee," "we always confirm," or satisfaction language. A copy change within 30 days of a review crisis is a tell.
3. Watch for a cluster of generic 5-star reviews from accounts with no other review history. Reputation management firms create these; they look like "Great job! Would recommend!" with no details.
4. Check Google Ads for competitor brand bidding — did they start bidding on "Forest City Power Washing" as a defensive move? This happens occasionally after a review-driven competitor attack.

**What still works from the Clean Pro attack:**
The core messaging that worked against Clean Pro's weakness — "flat-rate pricing, we confirm the day before, we show up" — remains strong brand language for Forest City. It's not attack copy anymore; it's a permanent differentiator that doesn't require a specific competitor to be broken. Keep it in rotation.

**Watch for:**

## The Three Opens — August 3

### Open #1 — Back-to-School Ownership (Now Through August 10, Highest Priority)

This is the most time-sensitive content window of the early August period. "School starts August 18" is a real, credible, local deadline. Any Forest City creative that uses that specific date has a built-in trust advantage over competitors running "before school starts" in generic terms. The window to run this with maximum impact is this week — by August 10, the deadline is close enough that some customers have already missed it; after August 15, it's academic.

**Check the Facebook Ad Library today (when running locally):** "power washing" in major NE Ohio ZIPs. Is anyone running school-year creative with a specific date? If not, Forest City has a clean lane. If so, identify who and what angle — can Forest City outflank with more local specificity or a stronger CTA?

**Action for Jasmine:** If "school starts August 18" creative isn't live yet, launch today. If it is live, check performance — this should be the top-performing ad this week.

### Open #2 — Brothers Overflow Capture (Ongoing Through Mid-August)

Brothers is almost certainly quoting 2–3 week waits for new inquiries. That puts the earliest opening at August 17–21 — past school start for most districts. A homeowner who specifically wants it done before school starts and hears that from Brothers is Forest City's customer. The conversion path is: Google search → Forest City GBP → strong reviews → call → Tommy offers a slot before August 18 → close.

This is a free customer acquisition channel — Brothers' quality reputation (which is high) has pre-sold the customer on the category. Forest City just needs to be visible, responsive, and available.

**Action for Rick:** Availability-intent keywords are live. Bid "power washing Cleveland this week" and "power washing Cleveland available August" if not running. Small volume, very high intent.

### Open #3 — Labor Day Outdoor Entertaining Frame (Opens Now, Peaks August 10–25)

Labor Day outdoor entertaining is the emerging urgency frame for homeowners without school-age kids. The audience is 35–65 homeowners, no school-age children, with a deck or patio. The hook is not "school starts in 2 weeks" — it's "Labor Day is 5 weeks out and your deck is going to be the center of attention."

This audience is slightly different from the back-to-school parent: less urgency about a specific date, more motivated by social appearance and the hosting experience. The Facebook creative should lean into the deck transformation and the social moment, not the countdown.

**Jasmine creative brief:** Before/after of a deck with "Labor Day is your last big outdoor entertaining weekend. Make the deck worth showing off." CTA: book before August 15 to guarantee Labor Day completion. Run August 1–20.
