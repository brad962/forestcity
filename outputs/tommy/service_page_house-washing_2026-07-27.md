## HOW WE DO IT DIFFERENTLY

### Soft Wash Isn't Just a Pressure Washer Turned Down

There's a reason we don't just point a pressure washer at your house and call it a day.

Soft washing uses low-pressure water with a biodegradable cleaning solution that kills the algae, mold, and bacteria at the root — not just knocking the top layer off the surface. That's the difference between results that last 2–3 years versus results that fade in 6 weeks.

We hear it all the time: "I didn't want to deal with the chemicals." Fair. But the solution we use is biodegradable — safe for your landscaping, your pets, your kids. We pre-wet every planted area before we start and rinse it again when we finish. We've been doing this long enough to know that a customer who's nervous about chemicals needs to see us being careful, not just hear that we are.

Here's what high pressure on siding actually does: it drives water behind the panels and into the wall cavity. That causes rot and hidden mold you won't find until it's a real problem. It strips paint on wood siding. It breaks old caulk seals around windows and trim. There's a reason professional house washers don't use high pressure on siding — it's not the right tool for it.

**What you get instead:**
- Algae and mold killed at the root — stays gone 2–3 years, not 6 weeks
- No damage to paint, caulk, window seals, or trim
- Safe biodegradable solution — your flowers, grass, and pets are fine
- We rinse landscaping before and after every single job
- You don't have to be home — just let us know if dogs are loose in the yard

## FAQ

**Q: Will the cleaning solution hurt my plants or lawn?**
No. We use a biodegradable soft-wash solution and we pre-wet all landscaping before we start. We've done this on hundreds of homes — gardens, flower beds, mulch, grass — and we rinse everything again when we're done. If you have something you're especially protective of, just point it out when we arrive.

**Q: I've heard soft wash chemicals can damage siding. Is that true?**
Pressure can damage siding — the wrong soft-wash solution can too, if the company doesn't know what they're doing. We've been doing this long enough to know which surfaces need diluted solution and which ones can handle standard mix. We're not just spraying and leaving. Every job gets checked before we start.

**Q: How long does it take?**
Most 3-bedroom single-story homes run 2–3 hours. A two-story with a detached garage might go 4 hours. We'll give you an honest estimate when you call so you can plan your day.

**Q: How long until I need to do it again?**
In Northeast Ohio, most homes go 2–3 years between washes. The lake humidity means algae grows faster here than most of the country. If you have a lot of trees close to the north side of the house or you're in a low-lying area, it can come back sooner. We'll tell you honestly when we see it.

**Q: My neighbor got theirs done and it looks incredible. Are you the same company?**
Could be — we do a lot of work in NE Ohio suburbs. Call or text 216-365-9212 and describe the neighborhood. We can usually tell you if it was us. If it was someone else, we'll still show up and do it right.

**Q: I tried a pressure washer last year and the green came back fast. Why would soft washing be different?**
Pressure only strips the top layer. The algae root is still in the siding and it regrows. Our solution kills it — same principle as bleach killing bathroom mold instead of just scrubbing the surface. That's why pressure-only results fade in weeks and soft wash results last 2–3 years.
