## PROBLEM SECTION

### You've Been Driving Past It All Summer. Now It's August.

You noticed it in May. You meant to do it in June. Now it's August and that dark staining on the north side of your roof is deeper, wider, and harder to ignore. Your neighbors can see it from the road. Your realtor mentioned it. You're done putting it off.

That black and dark gray streaking isn't dirt, age, or shingle failure — it's Gloeocapsa Magma, a type of algae that feeds on the limestone in your asphalt shingles. In Northeast Ohio, we're basically the perfect climate for it. Lake Erie humidity, shade from trees, warm summers — it grows fast and it doesn't stop. If you leave it, it retains moisture against your shingles, accelerates granule loss, and starts cutting years off your roof's lifespan.

The mistake most homeowners make — or the mistake their last cleaning company made — is using a pressure washer on the roof. One customer told us her neighbor's roof re-grew within a month after a pressure washing company came through. That's because pressure washing doesn't kill the algae. It just blasts the surface layer off while the roots stay in the shingle. Six weeks later, you're back where you started — except your shingles are now thinner and your warranty may be void.

Word's gotten around. More and more homeowners in NE Ohio already know: if someone shows up with a pressure washer and points it at your roof, send them home.

## HOW WE DO IT DIFFERENTLY

### Soft Wash Only. No Exceptions.

We don't pressure wash roofs. Not even on a light setting. Not at all.

Soft washing uses low-pressure water and a biodegradable cleaning solution that actually kills the algae — not just knocks it around. The solution breaks down the biological material at the root. The streaks are gone. The algae is dead. And your shingles are exactly as they were before we got there.

That's why it lasts. A soft wash in Northeast Ohio typically keeps a roof clean for 2–4 years. A pressure wash keeps it clean for 6 weeks. We've seen both outcomes firsthand. Every time someone calls us saying "I had it done last year and it already came back" — it was a pressure washer job.

**What the process looks like:**

- We pre-wet your gutters, downspouts, and landscaping before we start
- Apply the soft wash solution from the roof edge — controlled low-pressure application, minimal foot traffic on your shingles
- Let the solution dwell — it does the chemistry work so the rinse doesn't have to
- Low-pressure rinse from ridge to eave, directing runoff away from beds
- Final gutter flush to clear residue before we leave
- Before and after photos so you can see exactly what changed

**No warranty concerns.** The soft wash method we use is consistent with manufacturer cleaning recommendations for asphalt shingles. Pressure washing is what voids warranties. We've never had a customer come back to us with a manufacturer dispute over our process.

## FAQ

**Q: My neighbor said the company she used last year re-grew within a month. What did they do wrong?**
Almost certainly a pressure washer. Pressure washing removes the surface layer of algae but doesn't kill the root. The organism is still alive in your shingle — it just takes a few weeks to become visible again. Soft wash uses a chemical solution that kills the algae completely. That's why our results last 2–4 years instead of 2–4 weeks.

**Q: Will soft washing void my roof warranty?**
No — and that's actually the whole point. Pressure washing a roof is what voids most manufacturer warranties, because high-pressure water strips the protective granules off asphalt shingles. Soft washing uses pressure below the threshold that causes granule damage. Most manufacturers' warranty language either specifically allows soft washing or describes it as the correct cleaning method. If you have a warranty document you're concerned about, we're happy to look at the language with you before we quote.

**Q: My roof looks bad but I don't know if it needs cleaning or replacing. Which is it?**
A lot of roofs that look like they need replacing are just dirty. Dark streaking, greenish patches, and surface discoloration from algae look structurally bad but are surface-level. Once it's cleaned, homeowners are regularly surprised at how new the shingles look underneath. If there's actual physical damage — cracked shingles, failing flashing, buckling — that's a roofer issue. But if your roof is structurally sound and just looks terrible, a soft wash will change it completely. We can tell you from the quote call which situation you're likely dealing with.

**Q: How long will the results last in Northeast Ohio's climate?**
Usually 2–4 years. NE Ohio's humidity off Lake Erie is aggressive for algae growth, so we're at the shorter end of what you'd see in a drier climate. Factors that help: steeper pitch (water sheds faster), good southern sun exposure, fewer overhanging trees. We'll give you an honest read on your specific roof when we quote it.
