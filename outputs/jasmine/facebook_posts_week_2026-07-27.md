## Post 2 — Educational
**Suggested publish day:** Tuesday, July 29

---

Here's something most Northeast Ohio homeowners don't know: pressure washing your roof can void your warranty.

We get calls every summer from people who had a "deal" come out and blast the shingles with high pressure. It knocks around the algae — it doesn't kill it. It also strips granules off your shingles and can let water in behind the flashing.

Soft washing is the only right method for roofs. Low pressure + biodegradable solution that actually kills the bacteria at the root. The algae doesn't come back in 6 months. Your shingles stay intact. Your warranty stays valid.

Before you book anyone for a roof clean, ask what method they use. If they can't explain soft wash, hang up.
