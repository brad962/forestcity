## Post 2 — Educational
**No photo needed / use a graphic or roof close-up**

Thinking about renting a pressure washer and blasting your roof yourself? Stop right there.

Pressure washing a roof is one of the most common ways homeowners void their shingle warranty without realizing it. High-pressure water strips the granules off your shingles — those protect against UV damage and extend the life of your roof. Once they're gone, they don't come back.

The right method is soft washing: low pressure + biodegradable cleaning solution that actually kills the algae and bacteria at the root. The black streaks you're seeing? That's a living organism eating your shingles. Soft wash kills it. A pressure washer just knocks it around until it grows back.

We soft wash every roof we touch. That's not a preference — it's the only method that's safe for your home.
